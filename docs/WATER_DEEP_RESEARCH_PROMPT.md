# NSW-WAT-001 — Exhaustive engineering research brief for Repository Town water

**Research requirement:** produce dated, directly linked and reproducible NSW regional-town evidence. Start from Dubbo because independently documented intake → treatment → distribution and sewage → discharge/reuse are available, but benchmark Orange, Cobar/Nyngan, Broken Hill and any better documented candidate. Reevaluate choice based on a public-evidence matrix; never assert a street-level pipe map from regional schematics.

## Required deliverables (do not stop at a narrative essay)

1. **Source-to-tap trace:** catchment, river/bores, entitlements/allocations, raw-water intake/screens, river stage/drought, pumps, pipelines, raw tank, treatment process (exact *documented* technology per locality), coagulation/flocculation, sedimentation, filters, membranes only where applicable, disinfection and residual, clearwater storage, high-lift pumps, elevated reservoirs/pressure zones, trunk/reticulation, property connection, stop tap, meter, backflow control and internal cold/hot services.
2. **End-user flow:** building schedules per individual property (residential, clinic, school, cafe, bakery, university, factory, park and firefighting); toilets/cisterns, showers/baths, kitchens, washing machine, irrigation, trade wastes, leaks and meters. Differentiate potable, rainwater, recycled and private bore systems with no undocumented cross-connections.
3. **Wastewater all the way back:** traps/vents, drainage grade and branch connections, boundary trap/inspection opening as relevant, sewer service, gravity main, manholes, wet wells, sewage pumps/rising mains, inlet works/screenings/grit, primary/biological/tertiary steps as actually documented, disinfection as required, biosolids and lawful effluent discharge/reuse. Separate industry trade-waste controls and overflow incidents.
4. **Stormwater apart from sanitary:** roof gutters/downpipes, surface runoff, gullies/pits, underground culverts/drains, floodplain/creek outlets, detention, pollutant transport, surface infiltration and groundwater; distinguish separate sewer systems from combined systems (do not fabricate combined sewer for NSW town). Wet-weather inflow/infiltration to sanitary sewer is an abnormal process and requires modelling.
5. **Technical parameters:** pipe class, nominal/inside diameter, Hazen–Williams/Darcy–Weisbach headloss, static/elevation head, demand-dependent pressure, pressure zones, valve controls, PRVs, pump curves/efficiency/energy demand, water hammer exclusion, water age/quality limitations, Manning gravity sewer, inflow, wet-weather I&I, detention, pump stations, overflow/treatment envelopes.
6. **Dated equipment:** real Australian water pump, pipe, valve, backflow prevention, tank, treatment and sewer pump examples with manufacturer exact variant, source PDF/version/dimensions/rating, electrical load and permissible visual reuse; do not invent brands or connection sizes.
7. **Failure/recovery:** water-main break, leak, isolation, residual pressure, tank drawdown, disinfection incident/boil-water advisory distinction, drought rationing, contamination response, power outage, pump changeover/generator startup, backflow control, sewage blockage/surcharge, wet-weather flows, sewer spill, treatment failure, restoration sampling/clearance. Safety and advice must reflect public authority, never game diagnosis.
8. **Institutional rules:** WaterNSW vs council vs NSW Health vs EPA vs planning authorities, licence and monitoring reporting, PFAS updates with dates, Plumbing Code of Australia/NSW adoption and AS/NZS 3500 (edition to verify), Water Recycling Guidelines, trade-waste permits.
9. **Data inventory:** source URL, issuing agency, as-of period, dataset/API ID, metadata, measurement units, timezone, cadence, quality flags, licence, access conditions, confidentiality of critical infrastructure details and reuse permission. Do not import sensitive street-level assets from nonpublic source material.
10. **Open-source algorithms:** inspect actual EPANET engine + WNTR source and EPA SWMM solver, compare licensing, test fixtures and solver capabilities; separate pressure hydraulic network from sewer drainage, precipitation catchments and treatment kinetics. Compare game approaches (Cities: Skylines, Simutrans) and classify as game approximations, not engineering solvers.
11. **Repository Town audit:** 234 stable source/runtime site IDs from `audit/electricity/coverage.json` with evidence-based water/sewer service decisions; J8 Water Works and L8 Wastewater Works; cover public toilets/parks/industrial load and powered operations, topography and 3D pipes; avoid claims of actual Dubbo network geography.
12. **Formal model/acceptance:** versioned types/units/provenance, deterministic multi-layer directed graphs and conservation, isolated tests, EPANET/SWMM reference discrepancy, day/night/save/reload mobile performance, 30+ faults, actual hosted playtest, gate to deploy.
13. **Town-specific missing facts:** obtain actual water authority pipe plans only via permitted pathways; if unavailable, label synthetic network and create a source-dependent fixture. Never quietly interpolate sensitive asset locations.

**Report each finding as documented/inferred/synthetic, with page, units and date.** Deliver machine-readable evidence + a reproducible report, maps where legally permitted, and checksums for imported datasets. Flag unavailable evidence. The game must remain offline-playable from synthetic fixtures even if live telemetry disappears.
