# Source and evidence register — NSW regional electricity

**Research date:** 2026-10-25 (Australia/Sydney). Sources are externally maintained; verify archive/version when importing data. Each bullet gives **publisher/date/scope**, the public URL, what was actually supported, and a rights/verification warning.

## NSW network, planning and empirical data

1. **Essential Energy**, *Distribution Annual Planning Report 2025*, dated **22 December 2025**, p53 of 169 (PDF index page 52): schematic of Broken Hill, X2, Buronga, BHL32/BHL82, 66/33/22 kV lines and two 25 MVA turbine note, regional solar/wind nameplates; primary **network planning** evidence. https://www.essentialenergy.com.au/-/media/Project/EssentialEnergy/Website/Files/Our-Network/DAPR-Essential-Energy-2025.pdf?rev=929391b9ac8a4885b9fdca4e5a998b81. Diagram not to scale / not as-built pole map; map was visually inspected on PDF p53.
2. **Essential Energy**, *Zone Substation Reports*, latest listed historical dataset **2024–25** and previous years from **2012–13**: half-hourly kW/kVAr when available (published raw, not quality-assured by the utility), AEST timestamps; **historical empirical feeder/zone demand** source. https://www.essentialenergy.com.au/our-network/network-projects/zone-substation-reports. Import data in a follow-up task, record zone name and missing intervals.
3. **Menindee community infrastructure**, *Housing and Environmental Health Plan*, section **9.7 POWER AND STREET LIGHTING**, document date **5 July 2022**: supply route description, aerial services, pole lighting and town connection; primary locality description but historic, reconcile with current Essential Energy 2025 diagram. https://mpra.com.au/uploads/documents/archived/menindee_hehp_master.pdf.
4. **Essential Energy**, *Annual Report 2024–25*, network stats: >900k customers, 183k km overhead, 11k km underground, 1.4m poles, 360 zone substations; valuable scale checks, not individual Menindee asset count. https://eear25.bwdstrategic.com/pdf/Essential-Energy_Annual-Report_2024-25.pdf.

## Historical outages and backup supplies

5. **NSW DCCEEW**, *Far West NSW Major Power Outage – October 2024 Post Event Review Report*, **April 2025**, pp3, 7–9, 18–22: seven damaged towers, outage duration, backup turbine incidents, switching, demand shedding, generators, incidents and emergency response. https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-09/NSW-Far-West-Power-Outage-Review-Report.pdf. PDF event-timeline page 8 was visually inspected.
6. **NSW Parliament**, *The electricity outages affecting Far West NSW in October 2024*, **June 2025**, especially pp7–9: independent evidence of 24 October Pinnacles Place temporary microgrid and 26 October BESS reconnection. https://www.parliament.nsw.gov.au/ladocs/inquiries/3084/Report%20-%20The%20electricity%20outages%20affecting%20Far%20West%20NSW%20in%20October%202024%20%28June%202025%29.pdf. Search excerpt available; direct PDF fetch via web redirected and was not fully reviewed page-by-page.
7. **IPART NSW**, *Broken Hill Investigation Information Paper*, **December 2024**, p2: single 220 kV X2 national supply route, privately owned wind-farm line unusable during X2 outage, gas turbines and backup BESS; initial investigation rather than a full system schematic. https://www.ipart.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/cm9_documents/Information-Paper-Broken-Hill-Investigation-December-2024.PDF.
8. **NSW Government**, *Broken Hill and Far West communities back on the power grid*, **1 Nov 2024**, main line restoration/community chronology. https://www.nsw.gov.au/media-releases/broken-hill-and-far-west-communities-back-on-power-grid.
9. **Transgrid**, *New section of Broken Hill transmission line to go live*, c. May 2025: permanent 3.5 km 220 kV rebuilt section and changeover arrangements. https://www.transgrid.com.au/media-publications/news-articles/new-section-of-broken-hill-transmission-line-to-go-live/

## Batteries and community microgrids

10. **AGL**, *Broken Hill Battery Energy Storage System* (current project operations overview): **50 MW, one-hour** lithium-ion battery operational August 2024; list of technical/knowledge-sharing and operations CSV resources. https://www.agl.com.au/about-agl/operations/broken-hill-battery-energy-storage-system.
11. **ARENA / AGL**, *Broken Hill Battery Energy Storage System — Lessons Learnt Report #2*, dated **27 September 2024**, p7 (PDF index page 6): 50 MW / 50 MWh, 22 kV underground connection, **EPC Power** inverters, grid-forming/registration facts, AEMO full-capacity approval 21 Aug 2024. https://arena.gov.au/assets/2024/11/AGL-AGL-Broken-Hill-Grid-Forming-Battery-Lessons-Learnt-Report-2.pdf. Original AGL copyright; refer/cite, don't redistribute report content. This PDF page was visually inspected.
12. **ARENA**, *AGL Broken Hill Grid-Forming Battery*, project summary with project dates and aims. https://arena.gov.au/projects/agl-broken-hill-grid-forming-battery/.
13. **NSW Planning Portal**, *Broken Hill BESS*: approval record with **50 MW / 100 MWh development description** (not necessarily operating capacity); demonstrates why proposed vs installed must not be conflated. https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/major-projects/projects/broken-hill-battery-energy-storage-system.
14. **Endeavour Energy**, *Bawley Point and Kioloa Microgrid*, current public status and **Apr 2026** newsletter listing: approx 100 connected home systems and **3 MW grid battery**; network reliability and outage role. https://www.endeavourenergy.com.au/community/our-projects/bawley-point-microgrid.
15. **Endeavour Energy**, *Distribution Annual Planning Report 2024*, around p62: proposed/implemented **~3 MVA / 3 MWh BESS**, distributed energy resources management and smart metering. Public indexed PDF link https://www.endeavourenergy.com.au/__data/assets/file/0015/6360/DAPR-2024-Version-1.44.pdf currently returned 404 during retrieval; **do not treat unavailable direct download as page-verified.**
16. **Endeavour Energy**, *Bawley Point/Kioloa Microgrid FAQ*, regional end-of-line load and peak holiday tourism rationale. https://yoursay.endeavourenergy.com.au/bawley-point-kioloa-community-microgrid/widgets/368159/faqs.
17. **Endeavour Energy community newsletter**, **December 2025**, distinguishes Kioloa automatic outage restoration from Bawley/Termeil operator restoration; link hosted by local community site https://bawleykioloatermeil.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Bawley-Point-and-Kioloa-Microgrid-Newsletter-December-2025.pdf; official utility's site links newsletters; check most recent Apr 2026 for changes before designing present-day live logic.

## Source-quality notes

- All material is **publicly accessible** but not necessarily open-licensed; link and cite rather than redistribute diagrams or manufacturer reports.
- Case-study selection does not mean real breaker/transformer/control point coordinates are in this repo; no private asset inventories.
- Keep **source version and retrieval date** for later reproducibility; 2026 web pages may describe equipment not present in 2024.
- For exact equipment and compliance: **separate follow-up** with distributor connection guides, AS/NZS 3000, 4777.2 and 5033 as lawfully accessible, manufacturer variants, fuse/transformer ratings and operating envelope.
